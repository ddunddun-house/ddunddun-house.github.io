Chaekbangul Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-15. Same as Settings > Privacy Policy in the app.

1. Information collected: The operator does not collect any personal information. There is no sign-up or login.

2. Storage: Your library, reviews, saved lines and reading records are stored only on this device and in your own iCloud (Apple CloudKit private database). There is no operator server.

3. Book search: Book search is offered in the Korean version only. Only if you connect your own Kakao REST API key, the search terms you enter (title, author, ISBN) are sent to Kakao (Daum book search) to fetch book details and covers. The key is stored only in the device Keychain and is never sent to the operator. Your library and reviews are not sent.

4. Photos and camera: Text and barcodes in book page photos are read on the device (Apple Vision). Page photos are never saved or sent. Only cover photos you choose are saved, in reduced size.

5. AI: By default, on-device AI (Apple Foundation Models) or basic check rules are used, with nothing sent outside the device.

Only if you connect your own API key (Google Gemini or OpenRouter) and agree, your review text, one-line reviews and the book title are sent to that AI provider.

The key is stored only in the device Keychain and is never sent to the operator. AI usage fees may be charged to your own account under the provider's policy; the operator is not involved.

You can withdraw consent and disconnect at any time in Settings > AI.

6. Notifications: Reading and due-date reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device.

7. Photo library: Images and videos of reading cards and shelves are added only when you choose to save them. The app does not read your photo library.

8. Ads: The video ads that free users watch before showing off reading cards or shelves are provided by Google AdMob. When loading ads, Google may process device and app information, IP address and ad interactions (Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy). The app does not request ad tracking permission (IDFA) and does not contact ad servers before you open a show-off screen. Pro purchasers do not see ads.

9. Purchases: Pro subscriptions and lifetime purchases are processed by Apple; the operator does not receive payment information. Manage and cancel subscriptions in iOS Settings.

10. Deletion: Deleting the app or a book removes it from the device. iCloud data can be deleted in Settings > Apple Account > iCloud.

11. Contact: Please follow the instructions on the support page (https://ddunddun-house.github.io/chaekbangul/support.html).
