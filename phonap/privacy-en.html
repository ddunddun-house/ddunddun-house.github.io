PhonAp Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026 · Written: November 14, 2026 · Updated: November 25, 2026

PhonAp has no accounts and no servers. The developer receives no user data.

1. Information stored only on your device

| Item | Purpose | Where |

|---|---|---|

| Lock list (tokens encrypted by Apple; app names can't be read) | Blocking | Device (App Group) |

| Lockup, schedule, unlock and streak history; settings | Running the app, showing stats | Device (App Group) |

| Website addresses you add | Blocking | Device (App Group) |

| Lock Screen maker settings | Continue editing next time | Device |

This data is deleted when you delete the app. It may be included in your iCloud backup, but the developer can't access it.

2. Information used without being stored

  • Screen Time usage: shown only inside Apple's report extension and never leaves it.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: used only for the read-aloud mission, recognized on device. Never stored or sent.
  • Motion (step count): only the number is shown during the walking mission. Never stored or sent.
  • Photos: only "Add Photos" access is used, to save Lock Screen designs and stat cards. Your other photos are never read.
  • Show off my stats: the card image goes only to the app you pick in the share sheet, never to the developer.
  • AI lines: made by on-device AI on supported devices running iOS 26 or later. Nothing is sent out.

3. Ads (third party)

Free users see Google AdMob ads: a banner at the bottom of the Lock (idle), Schedules and Stats screens, and a rewarded ad when they tap "Watch an ad for +3 credits". There are no interstitial ads. We request non-personalized, general-audience ads only and never ask for tracking permission. Google may process device and usage information to serve ads and prevent fraud. See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Pro users see no ads.

3-1. Credits

Your credit balance and history (when and on which feature credits were used) are stored only on your device (App Group storage and Keychain) and are never sent to the developer. A copy stays in the Keychain so the balance carries over if you reinstall.

4. Payments

Payments are handled by Apple. The developer never receives payment details.

5. Children

PhonAp is a self-control app, not a parental monitoring app. It is not directed to children under 13.

6. Contact

Please contact us through the support page: https://ddunddun-house.github.io/phonap/support.html
