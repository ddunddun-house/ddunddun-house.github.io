MoaKok Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026 · Written: November 5, 2026 · Updated: November 14, 2026

1. Information we collect

The developer of MoaKok does not collect your personal information. There is no sign-up or login, and no developer servers or analytics.

However, the ad shown before sending a board in the free version is provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads and prevent fraud, Google may process device information (such as IP address, device and OS information, and ad interactions) under Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). MoaKok does not ask for tracking permission for personalized ads. Pro users and people who don't send boards never contact the ad servers.

2. Where your data is stored

  • Links, memos, chats, images, categories and keywords you save are stored on your device. Your custom category list stays on that device.
  • The app asks for camera access only when you take a photo for an image sticker. The photo is saved only in the sticker, not in your photo library, and is never sent to the developer.
  • If your device is signed in to iCloud, data is backed up and synced to your private iCloud database. The developer cannot access it.
  • When you use "Send the whole board", the board's contents (links, notes, photos, and chats if you choose to include them) are packed into one file and sent through the app you choose, such as Messages. It does not go through developer servers, and the recipient keeps a copy on their device.

3. Other people's messages

Chat stickers can include other people's names and messages. They are stored only as described in section 2 and are never sent to the developer. You decide what to save.

4. AI features

  • Default: on-device AI (Apple Foundation Models) and automatic rules. Nothing is sent off your device.
  • Optional: only if you register your own API key (for example Google Gemini, OpenRouter or OpenAI) and agree on the consent screen, the title, site name and description of saved links (up to 1,500 characters) are sent directly from your device to that provider.
  • Chats, memos, text in photos and your notes are not sent by default. They are sent (first 1,500 characters) only if you turn this on in Settings.
  • Content sent on the unpaid Google Gemini tier may be used by Google to improve its services and may be read by human reviewers, and Google's terms say not to submit sensitive, confidential or personal information. The Gemini API is for users 18 and older. Each provider's terms apply.
  • API keys are stored only in your device's Keychain and are never sent to or stored on developer servers. Fees may be charged to your own provider account under its policies; the developer does not pay or manage them.
  • You can disconnect and withdraw consent in Settings at any time.

5. Link previews

When you save a link, your device connects directly to that website (YouTube oEmbed for YouTube) to fetch the title, description and thumbnail.

6. In-app purchases

Pro subscriptions and lifetime purchases are processed by the Apple App Store. The developer does not receive payment information. You manage and cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account.

6-1. Clipboard and notifications

When you open the app, it checks on your device only whether what you copied is a link. It reads the content only when you tap Paste, then saves it as a sticker. Reminders are scheduled with this device's notification feature and are not sent to any developer server.

7. Contact

Please contact us through the support page: https://ddunddun-house.github.io/moakok/support.html
