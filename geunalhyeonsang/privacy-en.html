Develop Day Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 28, 2026

1. Data the developer collects: none. Develop Day has no sign-in, no developer server and no analytics. Your photos, roll names and notes are never sent to the developer.

2. Where data is stored: On your device. With "iCloud Backup" on (the default), roll data, including photos that have not developed yet, is also stored in your private iCloud storage, which the developer cannot access. You can turn it off in Settings at any time.

3. Camera: Used only to take photos for a roll. Scene brightness is calculated on the device from the camera's exposure values (aperture, shutter, ISO) and is not stored. The "Spring" film finds face positions on the device for skin smoothing; face data is never stored or sent. Photos are saved with the film look on the device (and in iCloud Backup) only.

4. Photos app: When you add from the library, only the photos you pick are passed to the app (the app does not ask for full library access). The "Add Photos" permission is used only to save developed photos to the Photos app.

5. Notifications: Develop-day reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device and send nothing out.

6. Widget: The next roll's name, develop day and shots left are stored in a shared space on the device (App Group) so the widget on the same device can show them.

7. Ads (free users): Only rewarded ads that you choose to watch are shown, when you use "Develop Now" or load a second or later roll (no banner ads). Google AdMob may collect the advertising identifier and device information. Where the law requires consent, an ad consent screen (Google UMP) appears first. No ads are shown after you buy Pro. See Google's privacy policy for details.

8. Purchases: Payments are handled by the Apple App Store. The developer does not receive payment information.

9. External AI: Not used.

10. Sharing: Photos, contact sheets and videos leave the app only when you send them yourself with the share sheet (Messages, KakaoTalk and so on).

11. Deleting data: When you delete a roll or a developed envelope, that roll's photos are deleted from the device and from iCloud. Deleting the app removes the data on the device; iCloud data can be deleted in Settings > Apple Account > iCloud > Manage Storage. Photos already saved to the Photos app are deleted in the Photos app.

12. Contact: Support page https://ddunddun-house.github.io/geunalhyeonsang/support.html
