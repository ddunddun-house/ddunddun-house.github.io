Munjang Jupjup Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-17 (v1.0). Same content as Settings > Privacy Policy in the app (English version).

1. Information collected: The developer does not collect any personal information. There is no sign-up or sign-in, and no analytics SDK is included. For ads, see item 8.

2. Where data is stored: Picked sentences, small copies of cropped photos, explanations and expressions, bookmarks, folders and quiz records are stored only on this device and in your own iCloud (Apple CloudKit private database). There is no developer server.

3. Camera and photos: The camera is used only when you photograph English sentences. Text in photos is read on the device (Apple Vision), and photos are never sent anywhere. From your photo library, only the photos you choose are passed to the app (system photo picker).

4. AI

  • By default, on-device AI (Apple Foundation Models) or built-in rules are used, with nothing sent off the device.
  • Only if you connect your own API key (Google Gemini or OpenRouter) and agree on the consent screen, the English sentence text you choose is sent to that AI provider (OpenRouter passes it to OpenRouter and the actual model provider). Photos are not sent.
  • The app may store your own API key for AI features. The key is stored only on your device (Keychain) and is never sent to or stored on the developer's servers.
  • AI requests go directly from your device to the provider with your key, and any usage fees may be charged to your own account under each provider's policy. The developer does not pay these costs or take part in this process.
  • With a free key, the provider may use the text you send to improve its services, and human reviewers may read it. Please don't include personal information in sentences you send. The provider's terms may require users to be 18 or older.
  • You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings > AI. When you disconnect, the key is deleted immediately.

5. Notifications: Bookmark reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device. Their content (English sentences) is never sent anywhere.

6. Widget: The list of sentences shown in the widget is kept only in the app group storage on the device.

7. Purchases: Pro purchases are handled by Apple. The developer does not receive payment information.

8. Ads: The video ad free users watch before showing off a sentence card is provided by Google AdMob. When an ad loads, Google may process device and app information, IP address and ad interactions (Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy). The app does not ask for tracking permission (IDFA), and it does not contact ad servers before you open the sentence card screen. Your sentences and photos are not used for ads. Pro users see no ads.

9. Deletion: Deleting a sentence or the app removes it from the device. iCloud data can be deleted in Settings > Apple Account > iCloud > Manage Storage.

10. Children: The developer does not collect children's personal information either. Ads are limited to the Teen content rating.

11. Contact: Support page https://ddunddun-house.github.io/munjangjupjup/support.html
